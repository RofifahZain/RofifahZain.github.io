Our Collections

Handcrafted contemporary fine jewellery from Milan, renowned for its coloured gemstones and elegant yet unconventional design.

An Icon Legacy

Since its inception in Milan in 1967, Pomellato has continuously pushed the boundaries of iconic style. From exquisite craftsmanship to magnificent jewelry, every creation remains handcrafted in-house.

Our Style

Eternally inspired by Milan’s strong creative spirit, Pomellato embodies a modern yet unconventional beauty that is deeply synonymous with its beloved hometown

Free Gems Philosophy

Rooted in the idea of freedom, Pomellato embraces a non-traditional design ethos known as “Free Gems.” This philosophy reflects the Maison’s mission to empower women while celebrating individuality.

Creativity

Pomellato was founded in Milan in 1967 from the visionary idea of entrepreneur Pino Rabolini, who sought to transform artisanal excellence into jewelry with designs that are both versatile and precious.

Story & Filosofi

1. The Art of Gems

Nature’s true artistry lies in its distinctive expressions. Each stone reveals its singular personality through revolutionary cutting techniques, including the patented 57-facet Nudo cut.

2. The Goldsmithing Tradition

Pomellato craftsmen often use microfusion, a technique that dates back to 3500 B.C. This process allows the creation of soft lines and voluminous, harmonious shapes that define the Maison’s signature style.

3. Made in Milan

Every day at Casa Pomellato in Milan, more than 150 artisans create extraordinary jewels. This process combines precious materials such as Pomellato’s signature rose gold, fine diamonds, and colored gemstones sourced from around the world.

The Art of Making

Every creation is brought to life by their precision and expertise, forged by countless hours of meticulous labor.

1. Sustainability Standards

Pomellato is part of the Kering Group, which implements a multifaceted strategy to achieve a more sustainable and responsible approach to luxury. This includes regularly reviewed codes related to human rights, biodiversity, and raw material sourcing.

2. Sustainable Sourcing and Annual Report

Since 2018, Pomellato has sourced gold through selected supply chains that meet the Kering Precious Metals Platform specifications. Approximately 95 percent of the gold used consists of recycled gold certified under the RJC Chain of Custody standard.

3. Artisan Craftsmanship and Tradition

Preserving the cultural and historical heritage of jewellery making is one of the company’s main motivations. Pomellato is committed to enhancing artisans’ skills through initiatives such as the Goldus Goldsmith Academy.

4. Environmental Profit and Loss

Every year, Pomellato prepares an Environmental Profit and Loss statement to measure carbon emissions, water consumption, and waste production across its entire supply chain. In 2022, the company successfully reduced its environmental impact by 83 percent.

Crafting Tomorrow, Today

Pioneering from the start, today Pomellato is a leading force for change sourcing 100% responsible gold.
